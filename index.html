How to Buy

Four steps. You buy $TITS with Robinhood ETH on Robinhood Chain. Uniswap handles the swap.

  1. 01

    Create your wallet

    Install MetaMask or another wallet that can reach Robinhood Chain. This wallet holds your Robinhood ETH and your $TITS.

  2. 02

    Get Robinhood ETH

    Fund the wallet with ETH on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood ETH is what you swap for $TITS and what pays the gas.

  3. 03

    Open the swap

    Hit Buy Now to open Uniswap on Robinhood with $TITS as the token you receive. Connect, stay on Robinhood, and paste the contract if it is not filled in.

  4. 04

    Swap and confirm

    Enter how much Robinhood ETH to spend, check the rate, and confirm in your wallet. When it lands, you are enrolled.
